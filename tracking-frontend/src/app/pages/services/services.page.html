FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTION

Have Any Question?

Answers to the most common questions about our shipping services.

Can I specify a delivery date when ordering?

Yes. During checkout, you can request your preferred delivery date. Starline Express will make every effort to schedule your shipment accordingly, depending on availability and destination.

What payment methods are supported?

We accept major credit cards, bank transfers and corporate invoicing for business accounts. Contact our team to discuss payment terms for recurring shipments.

What is the Federation of International Trade Association?

The Federation of International Trade Associations (FITA) is a global network that connects businesses involved in international trade. It provides resources, standards and networking for logistics providers worldwide.

How can I track my shipments?

Enter your tracking number on our Track Shipment page to view the latest recorded status, location and full tracking history of your package.
